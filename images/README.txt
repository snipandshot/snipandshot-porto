Put your photos here. One folder per album keeps things tidy:

  images/hero/01.jpg            (slideshow, ~1920px wide)
  images/clients-bg.jpg         (background behind the Clients box)
  images/boutique-store/cover.jpg
  images/boutique-store/01.jpg, 02.jpg, ...

Export tips: JPG or WebP, quality ~80%.
  Thumbnails/covers: 800px wide
  Album photos: 1600-2000px on the long side, under ~500 KB each
Avoid spaces in file names (use boutique-store, not "Boutique Store").
